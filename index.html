The Problem

Your pet's health data is scattered across vet offices, filing cabinets, and your memory.

Between vaccination dates, medication schedules, weight changes, and emergency contacts — most pet parents are guessing. FurēPET replaces the chaos with a single source of truth for your pet's complete health story.

📁

Medical Records

Store vaccinations, lab results, medications, and vet visit notes in one searchable history. Upload documents directly from your phone.

📈

Weight & Trends

Track your pet's weight over time with visual charts. Spot changes early and share insights with your vet for better care decisions.

👥

Care Village

Keep your vet, emergency hospital, groomer, and pet sitter contacts all in one place. When you need a number fast, it's right there.

🚨

Emergency CPR

Step-by-step CPR and first aid instructions for dogs and cats. When every second counts, FurēPET puts critical guidance at your fingertips.

🐾

Multi-Pet Support

Add all your pets to one account. Each gets their own profile, medical history, and health tracking — no matter how big your family gets.

🔒

Private & Secure

Your pet's health data is encrypted and never shared with advertisers. We store it securely so you're always in control.

Why We Built This
"After my dog Nina's health scare, I found myself drowning in 150+ pages of vet records with no way to organize them. I knew there had to be a better way."

FurēPET was born from a real pet parent's frustration. Built by a dog mom who needed it, designed for every pet parent who deserves better tools to care for the ones who can't speak for themselves.
